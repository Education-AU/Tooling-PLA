To use JetBrains products you must register at JetBrains as student using your student mail

https://www.jetbrains.com/academy/student-pack/

The JetBrains toolbox is a program to collectively control JetBrains products installed in your environment.

The ToolBox is downloaded from https://jetbrains.com/toolbox-app/

When you have installed this toolbox, you need at least two IDEs for this semester
1. Clion for C++ course
2. PyCharm for PLA course
